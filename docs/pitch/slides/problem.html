The problem: Portaldot apps live on two interfaces at once

Two views of one event

Solidity runs inside pallet-revive. Every event is an EVM log and a native Revive event. EVM tools miss dispatch errors and runtime versions; Substrate tools miss ABIs.

Fragile automation

Teams glue scripts, cron jobs and webhooks. A lost response or a crashed worker means a job runs twice — or never — and nobody can prove which.

Unclear costs and state

Storage deposits hide inside “gas”, 14-dp tPOT shows as 18 dp, and there is no event filter API. Deployments are hard to plan and to verify later.

Measured on the live V3 testnet, not assumed. The EVM endpoint is the pallet-revive eth-rpc proxy; eth_newFilter is unsupported; a 2 KB contract holds about 160 tPOT of storage deposit.
